My older brother and I played Starflight together when I was a kid, and 40
years later it is still my favorite game. I keep a browser port of the DOS
original around and give it a full playthrough every few years. It has the
best story twist I have ever seen in a video game, and I won't spoil it here,
because the game is four decades old and you should still play it.

Last night I asked an AI to build me a tribute to it while I slept, and I woke
up to a working game. We spent the morning polishing it together, and it now
has the mining, the crew training, the alien diplomacy, the starmap, and even
the hidden wormholes. If you ever played the original, it will feel like home.

It's free and runs in your browser: https://starflight-clone.vercel.app
